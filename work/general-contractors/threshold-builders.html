Process

How a project actually runs

The part homeowners are genuinely anxious about isn't the price. It's not knowing what happens or when.

  1. Consultation & feasibility

    We visit, listen, and tell you whether the idea works and roughly what it costs. Free, and sometimes the answer is don't.

  2. Design & fixed proposal

    Drawings, engineering where needed, and a line-item proposal — issued only once we both think the project is worth building.

  3. Permits & schedule

    We handle the city. You get a week-by-week schedule before anyone swings a hammer.

  4. Build & weekly updates

    A named project manager, a written update every Friday, and a final walkthrough with a punch list we actually close out.
