Process

How a project actually runs

The part that frightens people is unpermitted work surfacing when they sell. That is exactly the part we document.

  1. Consultation & feasibility

    We visit, listen, and tell you whether the idea works and roughly what it costs. Free, and sometimes the answer is don't.

  2. Design & fixed proposal

    Drawings, engineering where needed, and a line-item proposal. One number, with allowances stated openly.

  3. Permits & schedule

    We handle the city. You get a week-by-week schedule before anyone swings a hammer.

  4. Build & weekly updates

    A named project manager who pulls the permits in your name, schedules the inspections, and gives you the paperwork as it clears.
