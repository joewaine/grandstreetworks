Process

How it goes

No pressure appointments, no one-night-only pricing, no sales manager phoning from the car park.

  1. Estimate online

    Real numbers from your address and bill, instantly. No form gate, no phone call required to see it.

  2. Site survey

    We come out, get on the roof and check structure, shading and the panel. The estimate firms up or we tell you it doesn't work.

  3. Design, incentives, contract

    Final design with the current federal credit and local incentives applied, and financing if you want it.

  4. Install & switch on

    One to three days with our own crew. We handle interconnection with the utility and stay for monitoring setup.
