Process

Your first visit

About ninety minutes, most of which is talking rather than treating.

  1. Book online

    Real slots, real availability, no account required. Evenings and Saturdays included.

  2. Consultation

    Photographs, a conversation about what actually bothers you, and a plan. Sometimes the plan is ‘not yet’.

  3. Treat

    Same visit where it’s appropriate. You’ll know the exact cost before anything is drawn up.

  4. Two-week review

    Included, always. Neurotoxin settles over two weeks and small adjustments are free within that window.
