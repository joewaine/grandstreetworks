Twenty trades. Six directions each.

Reference builds, made to show what the method produces. The businesses are invented; the structure isn't. Each set started with the same read — what the best-performing firms in that trade put on a homepage, and what the rest leave off — and every direction fixes a named item from that trade's list.

They are six structurally different pages, not one layout in six colours. Open the closest one to your trade on a phone: that is the screen the argument is actually settled on.
