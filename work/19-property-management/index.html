Kestrel Property Group — six directions

Property management. Fictional company, real structure. Every direction is addressed to an owner asking what you would charge to manage his, and every one carries the owner statement — the door count and the fee table — where a tenant-facing site would put a rent-payment button. Open each on the phone: Get an owner proposal is reachable without scrolling in all six.
