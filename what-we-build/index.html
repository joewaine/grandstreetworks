[SEC_04] What we won't do

Build you an app you don't need

An app is right when the work happens away from a desk and away from signal. For most firms it's a more expensive website, and we'd rather say so before there's a deposit.

Anything compliance-heavy

Payroll, tax filing, accounting of record, clinical records, or anything putting protected health information through a system we built. That stays with the software carrying the liability.

Replace software that's good value

Plenty of what you pay for is cheap for what it does, or worth it because everyone else is on it. We'll say so.

Sell you a build the numbers didn't justify

If your site works and your week isn't drowning, we'd rather say so than invoice you for a project neither of us believed in.
