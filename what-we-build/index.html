[SEC_04] What we won't do

Build you an app you don't need

A native app is right when the work happens away from a desk and away from signal. For most practices and firms it is a more expensive way to have a website, and we'd rather say so on the first call than take the deposit.

Anything compliance-heavy

Payroll, tax filing, accounting of record, clinical records — anything that would put protected health information through a system we built. That stays with the specialists and the software carrying the liability.

Replace software that's good value

Plenty of what you pay for is cheap for what it does, or worth it because everyone else is on it. We'll say so, and it costs you nothing to hear it.

Sell you a build the numbers didn't justify

If your site is working and your week isn't drowning, we'd rather tell you and keep the relationship than invoice you for a project neither of us believed in.
