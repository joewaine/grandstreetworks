About

Seattle-raised, University of Washington trained, and happier on a trail than behind a desk.

My experience as a real estate broker in the Greater Seattle Area brings a wealth of local market knowledge to every transaction. Specializing in residential, investment, and new construction properties, my commitment to excellence and client satisfaction has made me an award-winning agent at Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices.

University of Washington alumni, with expertise in data analytics and market analysis. I have lived in Seattle for most of my life, and have always loved exploring the diverse neighborhoods and communities. I regularly walk the Pacific Northwest's scenic trails with my wife and two dogs.

If you're thinking about making a move, let's explore today's dynamic real estate market and find the best approach tailored to your needs.
