How a job runs

We care about the small details.

Dealing with home construction can be overwhelming. Our job is to run it smoothly so you have peace of mind from A to Z.

  1. 01

    Permit acquisition and preparation

    We pull the permits and prepare the paperwork before anything is built.

  2. 02

    Project planning and design

    Layout, materials and budget agreed with you first. Our process begins with listening.

  3. 03

    Permit expediting and inspection scheduling

    We schedule and attend the inspections so the job keeps moving.

  4. 04

    Final inspection and project completion

    Signed off, cleaned up, and we check in afterwards to make sure everything is working.
