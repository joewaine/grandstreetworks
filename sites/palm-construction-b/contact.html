Before you call

Common questions.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. Permit acquisition, expediting and inspection scheduling are the first and third steps of every job we run.

How soon can a project start?

Most projects can start within a week of signing, depending on permits and materials.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We are partnered with financing firms that can cover up to 100% of the project.

What materials do you use for decks and patios?

Wood, composite and pavers, depending on the project. We are a TrexPRO Platinum partner for composite decking.

Do you provide warranties?

Yes. Flooring installations carry a warranty, and Trex decks carry the manufacturer's warranty through our installer certification.

Can you work within my budget?

Absolutely. We work closely with clients to create remodel plans that align with their budget.
