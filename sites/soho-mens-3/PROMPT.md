## The direction — "The Member's Rooms"

**Thesis:** The practice as a discreet members' club — hospitality-led, warm and
tactile; booking an appointment feels like being expected, not processed.

- **Brand adjectives:** Warm · Tactile · Personal
- **Why it fits:** Privacy anxiety is the biggest barrier in men's health. A
  hospitality frame ("your visit, hosted") lowers it, and the concierge tone
  matches out-of-network premium pricing.
- **Typography & colour:** Soft text serif (Newsreader) with a humanist sans for
  UI; warm ivory, espresso and oxblood accents over a sage base; generous
  rounded forms and soft shadows.
- **Image / art direction:** Warm-graded crops in rounded frames, assembled in
  loose, collage-like compositions. Emphasis on hands, rooms and welcome rather
  than devices.
- **Signature idea:** A concierge booking strip that follows the narrative
  ("When would you like to come in?"), and testimonials presented as a guest book.

**The risk you must design against:** this is the most cliché-prone direction
available. Spa/lifestyle softness can undersell surgical authority and drift
into generic wellness branding. The founder is a surgeon-scientist (MD, PhD,
MBA, FACS). Warmth must not cost credibility — if a choice reads as "day spa"
rather than "private practice", it is wrong. Say in your report how you held
that line.
