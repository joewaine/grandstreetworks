# Essential Fence Center — client site

A one-page site for Essential Fence Center, LLC of Snohomish, WA (Randy and
Sue Cole), replacing their Thryv-built site at essentialfencecenter.com. Built
from the Grand Street Works trade set, but it is a **real client site**, not a
reference build: no `noindex`, no "fictional" footer, and it lives here under
`sites/` rather than `work/` so it can be lifted onto their own domain
unchanged.

Generated by `tools/build-fencing-site.py`. **Do not edit `index.html` by
hand** — change `CONFIG` or the copy tables in the script and rerun:

    python3 tools/build-fencing-site.py --page     # rewrite index.html only
    python3 tools/build-fencing-site.py            # also re-encode images, identity

## Where the content came from

Everything factual is theirs, lifted from essentialfencecenter.com on
2026-08-28 (they said the content could be used):

- Name, phone, email, street address, hours, payment methods, Facebook and
  Google links: their site header, footer and contact page.
- "Fencing done right" (the headline) and the mission line in the closing
  band ("bring the family back to their back yard paradise"): their homepage.
- Services: their nav and service pages, plus the "Other services" page
  (Ghost Controls gate operators, steel-frame gates, dog kennels, DIY
  material packages, cedar garden boxes and bird houses).
- About copy (45+ years, crews with 30+, hand-picked material, "50+ years
  experience" on posts): their About and FAQ pages.
- The FAQ: ten of their fourteen questions, lightly edited.
- The three reviews: verbatim from their homepage (Hana S. is an excerpt).
- The service-area towns: their contact page, in their order.
- L&I registration **ESSENFC796CE**: from the WA L&I register via a
  contractor-index listing (active, issued 2021-02-19, expires 2027-02-19).
  Confirm it once against lni.wa.gov before launch; it is on the page and
  Washington requires it in advertising.
- The mark: their red "EFC" roundel (`logo.png` on their site, 200px),
  redrawn as SVG. The accent colour is sampled from it.

## Photographs

The gallery and the About photograph are **their own job photos**, pulled
from their gallery page with their captions (originals in
`~/fractal/clients/essential-fence-center/real/`). They are phone photos of
varying size; the encoder never upscales, so smaller ones get only the rungs
of the AVIF ladder they can fill.

Two backdrops are **generated** (Gemini, `tools/gen-fencing-images.py`): the
hero (a craftsman home with a cedar picket fence) and the closing band (a
farmhouse with a cedar privacy fence). They are atmospheric rather than
portfolio, but they are not fences EFC built. If Randy has a good wide shot
of a finished job in front of a house, swap it in: drop it in the originals
folder as `hero.jpg` and rerun. Same for `farmhouse.jpg`.

## Before launch

- **Confirm with the owners**: the L&I number, that `rlcole2257@gmail.com`
  is where estimate requests should go, hours, and that the discounts (senior,
  veteran, "say you saw us on the website") are still offered.
- **The estimate form** has no backend. With `quote_endpoint` empty it opens
  the visitor's mail client with the fields filled in (and does the same
  without JavaScript, via `mailto:` + `enctype=text/plain`). Point it at a
  real endpoint — the intake API in `intake-api/` would do with a second
  route, or Formspree/Basin — and the JS posts JSON there instead.
- **Their domain.** When they are ready, point essentialfencecenter.com at a
  Render static service publishing this folder (or copy the folder anywhere
  that serves files), and set `CONFIG["url"]` to the real domain so the
  `og:image` and JSON-LD URLs follow. One Google Fonts request (Barlow,
  Barlow Condensed) is the only external dependency.
