[SEC_07] What we won't do

Build you an app you don't need

A native app is right when the work happens away from a desk and away from signal. For most practices and firms it is a more expensive way to have a website, and we'd rather say so on the first call than take the deposit.

Anything compliance-heavy

Payroll, tax filing, accounting of record, clinical records — anything that would put protected health information through a system we built. That stays with the specialists and the software carrying the liability.

Replace software that's good value

Plenty of what you pay for is cheap for what it does, or worth it because everyone else is on it. We'll say so, and it costs you nothing to hear it.

Sell you a build the numbers didn't justify

If your site is working and your week isn't drowning, we'd rather tell you and keep the relationship than invoice you for a project neither of us believed in.

[SEC_10] Fair questions
Our site was fine when we paid for it.

Most of them were. What changed isn't taste — it's that the majority of the people checking you out now do it on a phone, in the evening, with two of your competitors open in other tabs. A page that was reasonable on a desktop in 2016 loses that comparison on load time alone, before anyone reads a word of it.

We're too small for this.

Smaller usually makes the maths better, not worse. In a ten-person firm one person is doing four jobs, so an enquiry that books itself is an hour returned to four functions. The businesses that get least out of this are the large ones with a marketing team already asking the same questions.

Why would you rebuild our homepage for nothing?

Because it's a better first conversation than a sales call, and it's cheap for us to be wrong. If the rebuild is obviously better than what you have, you'll want to talk about the rest of it. If it isn't, you've lost an email and we've lost an afternoon. There's no invoice attached to it and nothing to cancel — you can hand the whole thing to the developer you already use.

Is our client data safe?

Anything we build runs on your accounts and your hosting, and nothing gets write access until you've watched it work with drafts and approvals first. We stay out of clinical records, payroll and anything else carrying regulatory liability, and any system that touches your client list comes with a written rule for your team about what can and can't leave it.

Aren't you just a new kind of lock-in?

You own the repository, the data and the infrastructure accounts, and that's in the contract rather than in a sales conversation. The care plan is optional and declining it doesn't cost you access to anything. If you want to move to another engineer, everything they need is already in your possession. Replaceable by design.

What does it cost, and how much of our time does it take?

Everything is a fixed fee agreed before work starts — no hourly billing and no scope-creep conversations. Your side of a site build is one call to pick a direction, one pass on the words, and one review before it goes live. The free rebuild costs you an email.
