[SEC_05] Fair questions
Our site was fine when we paid for it.

Most were. But people look you up on a phone now, in the evening, with two competitors open in other tabs. A page built for a 2016 desktop loses on load time alone.

We're too small for this.

Smaller usually makes the maths better. One person in a ten-person firm is doing four jobs, so an enquiry that books itself hands an hour back to all four. Big firms with a marketing team get the least out of this.

Is our client data safe?

Anything we build runs on your accounts and your hosting, and nothing gets write access until you've watched it work on drafts. We stay out of clinical records, payroll and anything carrying regulatory liability.

Aren't you just a new kind of lock-in?

No. The code, the data and the accounts sit in your name from day one, and the contract says so. The care plan is optional. If you ever hand the site to another engineer, they'll find everything already in place and nothing to prise out of us.

What does it cost, and how much of our time does it take?

Fixed fee agreed before work starts, no hourly billing. Your side of a build is one call to pick a direction, one pass on the words, one review before it goes live. The homepage rebuild takes an email.
